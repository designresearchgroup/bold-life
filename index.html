The founder

Meet Jamesyn.

Strategic guidance for ambitious women navigating career turning points.

For more than two decades I’ve worked at the intersection of leadership, technology, and professional development — building teams, developing talent, and helping high performers grow. And I kept noticing the same pattern: capable women reaching a point where the career that once fit them no longer did. From the outside, everything still looked successful. Inside, they were asking a quieter question — what’s next for me? Not for lack of ambition or capability, but because they’d outgrown something and had no clear process for what to do next.

I know it because I’ve lived it. I left teaching — a career I’d worked hard to build and genuinely loved — because I could feel it no longer fit who I was becoming. From there I built a career in corporate learning and development, then people leadership and senior management in MedTech: nearly a decade, and a complete identity shift from individual contributor to executive leader. Then, even as it was succeeding on paper, another pull emerged — to build something of my own, and to help other women through the same turning points. That’s why I created The Bold Life.

At every stage, the hardest part was never making the move. It was figuring out what was actually wrong, what I actually wanted, and what the right next step actually was — without a process to guide the decision. That’s what I build for the women I work with.

Most career advice jumps straight to solutions — quit, switch companies, start something new. But without understanding the real signal behind the frustration, those moves tend to recreate the same problem somewhere else. Sometimes it’s burnout, sometimes a plateau, sometimes misalignment with the culture or leadership — and sometimes you’ve simply outgrown where you are. The work is to diagnose the signal before you make the move.
